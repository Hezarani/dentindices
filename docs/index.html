Dentition status (WHO 2013)

Pick a code, then tap teeth to apply it. Tap a tooth again with the same code to clear it. Primary teeth use letters A–G.

Codes 6 (sealant), 7 (crown/abutment/implant), 8 (unerupted) and 9 (not recorded) are not counted. WHO 2013 counts code 5 (missing for another reason) in M from age 30.

Periodontal screening

Periodontitis case definitions from a six-site chart

Enter probing depth (PD) and clinical attachment level (CAL) in millimetres and tick bleeding on probing, for MB, B, DB (buccal) and ML, L, DL (lingual/palatal). Mark missing teeth. Third molars are excluded, as both definitions require.

ACES details

Needed for gingivitis vs health (bleeding), grade (age, bone loss, smoking, diabetes) and, in planned surveys, tooth loss and complexity factors.

Oral hygiene and gingival indices

Basic Erosive Wear Examination (BEWE)

Highest score per sextant from the buccal, occlusal/incisal and lingual/palatal surfaces: 0 no wear · 1 initial loss of surface texture · 2 distinct defect, hard-tissue loss < 50% of the surface · 3 loss ≥ 50%. Third molars only when they replace a second molar.

Dean's index — one person

Score the two most affected teeth (WHO 2013 codes). If they differ, the person's score is the less affected of the two.

Community Fluorosis Index — a group

Number of people in each Dean category (codes 8 and 9 left out).

Dental Aesthetic Index (DAI)

WHO 1997 regression equation. Measure with the CPI probe and record whole millimetres. Intended from about age 12.

Whole-survey processing

Load a CSV file with one row per person (tooth columns named by FDI number such as 16 or 55; sextant columns UR UA UL LL LA LR or S1–S6), or a long-format periodontal chart (id, tooth, site, pd, cal, bop). Everything is computed on this device; the file is never uploaded.

Indices and their sources

Each index was checked against its source: primary means the original paper or manual was read; secondary means the original is not openly available in full and the rule was checked against its abstract and/or independent publications that restate it.
